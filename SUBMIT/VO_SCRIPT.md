# Voiceover script (voice: "Danish" on ElevenLabs), target 2:40

Every fact comes from FACTS.md. [Brackets] are picture cues and are not read aloud.

---

**[1 · Hook: text cards over the living room]**
Babies start understanding words at six months.
Nine in ten Deaf babies are born to hearing parents.
So for a lot of families, their baby's first language is one they've never learned.

**[2 · The phone problem]**
Today that means learning from a phone app.
A tiny screen, one hand holding the phone, one person at a time.
And nobody to tell you when your sign is wrong.

**[3 · Phone on the stand, QR scan, "Phone connected"]**
This is First Signs, running on Fire TV.
Your phone becomes the camera, so both hands are free,
and the whole family learns on the biggest screen in the house.

**[4 · Main shot: milk, a miss, a retry, a ✓]**
Every sign is shown life-size by a Deaf signer.
Then it's your turn.
First Signs watches your hands and tells you, right away, whether you got it,
and if not, what it looked like instead.

**[5 · Talk with Baby]**
Talk with Baby puts the words into real moments.
Baby's reaching for the table. What do they want?
You answer in sign, and the TV answers you back.

**[6 · Sign Together / Family round, if filmed]**
Two people can sign at once, and everyone sees the feedback.

**[7 · Diagram + accuracy card]**
The hand tracking runs on the phone. Your video never leaves it; only hand points are sent to the TV.
Our recogniser was trained on Deaf signers, then tested on ten Deaf signers it had never seen.
Within a pack, it gets the sign in its top three ninety-seven point seven percent of the time.
Sixty-one first signs, across six everyday moments.
And it runs natively on Vega OS, Amazon's newest TV platform, on a real Fire TV Stick.

**[8 · Respect + what's next]**
First Signs doesn't replace Deaf teachers, and it points families to Deaf-led classes.
With or without an implant, signing means your baby always has a language.
Next: more packs, devices with built-in cameras like Echo Show, and Auslan, built with Deaf Australians.

**[9 · Logo]**
First Signs. Your baby's first words, together.
